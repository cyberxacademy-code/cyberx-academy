ABOUT CYBERX ACADEMY

Learn. Practice. Secure.

CyberX Academy is a cybersecurity learning platform focused on helping beginners understand technology, develop practical skills and build a strong foundation for a cybersecurity journey.

WHO WE ARE

Cybersecurity education made easier.

Cybersecurity can feel complicated when you don't know where to start. CyberX Academy is designed to make the learning journey structured, understandable and practical.

🛡️

Our Mission

Our mission is to make cybersecurity education accessible to learners who want to build strong technical foundations and understand how modern systems, networks and security technologies work.

We believe cybersecurity should be learned through understanding, practice and continuous improvement — not simply memorizing tools and commands.

🎯

Our Approach

CyberX Academy follows a structured learning approach: start with fundamentals, understand the concepts, practice safely and gradually build more advanced skills.

The goal is to help learners become confident enough to explore cybersecurity responsibly and continue developing their skills independently.

WHAT WE TEACH

Build the right foundation.

Our learning content covers the technologies and concepts that form the foundation of cybersecurity.

💻

Computer Fundamentals

Understand hardware, operating systems, files, processes and basic system concepts.

🌐

Networking

Learn IP addressing, DNS, DHCP, TCP/IP, ports, protocols, routers and firewalls.

🐧

Linux

Develop Linux command-line skills and understand permissions, processes, files and system administration.

🔐

Cybersecurity

Explore threats, vulnerabilities, authentication, malware, security principles and defensive concepts.
